YMM4M 0.1.0 development build 8

This disk image is for local development evaluation only.

- The app has only a linker-generated ad-hoc signature.
- It is not signed with Developer ID and is not notarized.
- It is not a release-ready distribution.
- YukkuriMovieMaker4, Wine, DXMT, Microsoft runtimes/fonts, and CrossOver are
  not included.
- YukkuriMovieMaker4 must be obtained separately from its official source.
- Compatibility is incomplete. Do not use this build with an irreplaceable
  project; work on an isolated copy.

Known incomplete gates include Windows-reference comparison, audible playback,
VoiceItem workflow with a licensed supported engine, Win32Service crash
isolation, extended stability/soak and fault-recovery testing, drag-and-drop,
clipboard, signing, notarization, and clean-machine validation.

YMM4M is unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the developer of
YukkuriMovieMaker4.

Setup instructions are included in `MACでYMM4を開く手順.md`.
